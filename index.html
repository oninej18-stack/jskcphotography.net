02 — BEHIND THE CAMERA

THE PERSON
BEHIND THE LENS.

I'm Jhi'Xe Young, a 17-year-old high school senior and the photographer behind JHI SHOOTS KC. What started as a curiosity for exploring the world around me grew into a passion for documenting it. I'm drawn to people, places, cultures, and the little details that often go unnoticed. Whether I'm photographing everyday life, capturing an event, or exploring somewhere new, I want my work to preserve moments and tell stories through my own perspective. Photography allows me to connect with the world differently. It's more than taking pictures; it's about observation, storytelling, and finding meaning in the moments happening around us. This archive is a growing collection of my work, my experiences, and the world as I see it.
